The Good News
“For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.” – Romans 3:23

The Bible is very clear on this point. We are all sinners. That means we have broken God's perfect law and are by nature in rebellion against Him.

“For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.” – Romans 6:23

Because of our sin, we are guilty before God and deserve His judgment. The Bible uses the term "wages" to describe something that we have earned by our sin. We have earned death as the just consequence for our sin.

“But God shows His love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.” – Romans 5:8

There it is, the greatest news you will ever hear. God loves us and has proven it in the greatest act of love the world has ever seen. Even when we were still His enemies, Jesus died in our place.

And his death was not just a tragic accident or unexpected mistake. This was God's plan to save us from the beginning. In fact, the Bible contains descriptions of this plan centuries before it took place. One of the clearest pictures is from the prophet Isaiah, who's words were written down 700 years before Jesus's death on the cross. Look at how remarkably Isaiah's words point to what Jesus would accomplish for us on the cross:

Surely he has borne our griefs and carried our sorrows;
yet we esteemed him stricken, smitten by God, and afflicted.
But he was pierced for our transgressions;
he was crushed for our iniquities [sins];
upon him was the chastisement that brought us peace,
and with his wounds we are healed.
All we like sheep have gone astray;
we have turned—every one—to his own way;
and the LORD has laid on him the iniquity [sin] of us all.
– Isaiah 53:4-6

In His death on the cross, Jesus took the wages that we had earned through our sin, and He bore them Himself.

So how do we respond?

“If you declare with your mouth, ‘Jesus is Lord,’ and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved.” – Romans 10:9
“For everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.” – Romans 10:13
“If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.” – 1 John 1:9

Call out to Jesus. Humbly confess your sin. Believe in Him and that His sacrifice on the cross was for you. Trust in His promise to save.

“Therefore, since we have been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.” – Romans 5:1
“There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.” – Romans 8:1

These verses are full of incredible promises for those who are trusting in Jesus. Think about how amazing this is: there is no condemnation, and we have peace with God.

One of the ways the Bible describes the Christian life is as a race. We know our destination, but we haven't arrived there yet.
